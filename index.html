About Me

I'm a fourth-year Computer Science student BS/MS at UC San Diego with a passion for data science, natural language processing, and healthcare technology. My experience spans machine learning, NLP research, web development, and data visualization, with a focus on building impactful solutions for the medical device industry.

Currently, I'm working as a Data Science and Natural Language Processing Intern at Edwards Lifesciences, where I develop AI-driven solutions for medical device analytics and patient outcomes research. I was also a tutor for various classes at UC San Diego and worked as a Residential Assistant for Rita and Revelle College at UC San Diego.

My previous experience includes VR data visualization work with the Qualcomm Institute, UI development and accessibility work at Cornerstone OnDemand, full-stack development for ACM UCSD's membership portal serving 6000+ users, and research in natural language processing that resulted in a first-author publication at IEEE ISTAS 2024.

I thrive in interdisciplinary teams, combining technical expertise with a collaborative mindset, and I'm always eager to tackle new challenges in emerging technologies.
