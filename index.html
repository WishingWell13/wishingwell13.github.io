About Me

I'm a software engineer and researcher with a passion for mechatronics, autonomous systems, and data-driven solutions. My experience spans robotics, web development, and natural language processing, with a focus on building impactful, scalable technology.

I thrive in interdisciplinary teams, combining technical expertise with a collaborative mindset. My work includes developing real-time autonomous vehicles, visualizing scientific data in VR, and teaching computer science at UC San Diego.

I'm always eager to tackle new challenges, learn emerging technologies, and contribute to projects that make a difference in the world.
