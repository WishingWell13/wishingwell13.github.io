About Me

I'm a Data Scientist II at Edwards Lifesciences and a UC San Diego M.S. Computer Science graduate specializing in AI and machine learning. My work spans machine learning, NLP research, web development, and data visualization, with a focus on building impactful solutions for the medical device industry.

I develop AI-driven solutions for healthcare and medical device analytics, including multimodal document extraction and human-in-the-loop validation workflows. I also enjoy teaching, contributing to open source, and researching how technology can better serve people.

My previous experience includes VR data visualization work with the Qualcomm Institute, UI development and accessibility work at Cornerstone OnDemand, full-stack development for ACM UCSD's membership portal serving 6,000+ users, and research resulting in publications at IEEE ISTAS, Computing in Cardiology, ITiCSE, and ASSETS.

I thrive in interdisciplinary teams, combining technical expertise with a collaborative mindset, and I'm always eager to tackle new challenges in emerging technologies.
